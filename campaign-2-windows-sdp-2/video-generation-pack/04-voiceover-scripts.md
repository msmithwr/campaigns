## Voice Direction

English:

Human, consultative, and calm. It should sound like a senior Cloudwrxs consultant speaking to a CTO or CFO, not like a generic explainer video. Use a neutral British or international business accent. Add small natural pauses after key ideas. Avoid an over-polished "radio advert" tone. Keep the energy warm, confident, and conversational.

Arabic:

Professional Modern Standard Arabic, but natural and human rather than formal broadcast news. Clear technical pronunciation for AWS, SDP, Windows Server, and Cloudwrxs. Executive tone, measured pace, warm delivery, with natural pauses. Avoid exaggerated promotional emphasis.

## Voice Production Recommendation

Best quality:

- Use a real human voiceover for the final cut, especially for the Arabic version.

Good fast option:

- Use a high-quality voice generation tool for the narration only, then assemble it with the Grok visuals.

Avoid:

- Do not use Grok's auto-generated video voice if it sounds synthetic, rushed, or detached from the pacing of the edit.
- Do not use voiceover with heavy music or sound effects baked in.

## Direction To Give A Voice Artist Or Voice Tool

Read as if you are explaining this to a business leader in a short client meeting. Keep it clear, measured, and credible. This is not a hype video. It should feel like a practical invitation to understand the savings opportunity.
